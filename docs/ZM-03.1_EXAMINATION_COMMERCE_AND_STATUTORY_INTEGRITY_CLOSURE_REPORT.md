## 3. Workstream Remediation & Architecture Verification Register

### Workstream A: Examination Attempt Integrity & Frozen Snapshots
- **Root Cause Addressed:** Previously, `ExamAttempt.assignedQuestionIds` only recorded question ID references. If an instructor or administrator edited a question stem, answer options, or correct option IDs in the authoritative question bank while a student was actively sitting an examination, the candidate's active attempt and post-exam scoring were susceptible to live mutation.
- **Architectural Fix Implemented:**
  - Introduced `FrozenExamQuestionSnapshot` interface in `src/types/index.ts`.
  - Added `questionSnapshots?: FrozenExamQuestionSnapshot[];` and `blueprintVersion?: number;` to `ExamAttempt`.
  - In `ZealoshDatabase.startExamAttempt`, each assigned question is deeply cloned into an immutable snapshot including question stem, options, points, rubric criteria, correct option IDs, and official explanations.
  - In `ZealoshDatabase.getQuestionsForAttempt`, client questions are projected directly from the attempt's frozen snapshots, strictly redacting `correctOptionIds`, `explanation`, and `referenceSource`.
  - In `ZealoshDatabase.submitExamAttempt`, scoring evaluates exclusively against the frozen snapshots. Even if the question bank is mutated or deleted after attempt start, candidate attempts remain immutable and score accurately.
  - Server-authoritative countdown timer deadline enforces the configured grace period (e.g. 60s or 120s). Submissions or in-flight autosaves following deadline expiry are strictly rejected with `EXAM_DEADLINE_EXPIRED`.
  - Foreign key cascade hazard in `supabase/migrations/20261031000011_zealosh_exam_and_commerce_phase3.sql` remediated: changed `attempt_id`, `question_id`, and `user_id` from `ON DELETE CASCADE` to `ON DELETE RESTRICT` on `exam_manual_evaluations` to protect historical evaluation records against accidental attempt purging.

### Workstream B: Statutory Indian GST Determination Engine (CBIC Compliant)
- **Root Cause Addressed:** GST was previously calculated via hardcoded 18% or 0% checks. The Letter of Undertaking (LUT) ARN was hardcoded as a constant reference (`AD320326000142L`) without verifying export conditions or LUT validity.
- **Architectural Fix Implemented (`src/lib/tax/tax-engine.ts`):**
  - Implemented authoritative `TaxProfile` and decoupled LUT reference via `process.env.STATUTORY_LUT_ARN` and validity window (`lutValidFrom`, `lutValidUntil`, `lutVerified`).
  - Implemented 5-condition export check per **Section 2(6) of the IGST Act, 2017**:
    1. Supplier is located in India (Zealosh, Kollam, Kerala, State Code 32).
    2. Recipient is located outside India.
    3. Place of supply is outside India per Section 13(2) of the IGST Act.
    4. Payment is received in convertible foreign exchange (USD, EUR, GBP; payment in INR is rejected as export).
    5. Supplier and recipient are not merely establishments of a distinct person per Section 8 of the IGST Act.
  - Implemented `roundFinancial` helper enforcing strict 2 decimal places (paise/cents) rounding, eliminating IEEE 754 floating-point drift.
  - Implemented tax determination rules:
    - **Intra-State Supply (Kerala, State 32):** 9% CGST + 9% SGST. Exact sum preservation guarantees `cgstAmount + sgstAmount === totalTax`.
    - **Inter-State Supply (Other Indian States):** 18% IGST.
    - **Zero-Rated Export of Services under LUT:** 0% GST with statutory LUT ARN reference attached.
    - **Taxable Export of Services:** 18% IGST when export conditions are satisfied but LUT is expired, missing, or unverified.
    - **Fail-Closed Tax Review:** If billing geography is missing or foreign buyer pays in domestic INR, transaction is classified as `TAX_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

### Workstream C: Invoicing & Credit Note Statutory Compliance
- **Root Cause Addressed:** Invoices and credit notes must comply with statutory serial numbering limits and relationship mapping.
- **Architectural Fix Implemented:**
  - Implemented strict CBIC **Rule 46(b)** serial numbering limit: `INV-YYYY-XXXX` sequence numbers are validated to ensure total length does not exceed 16 characters. An explicit error (`STATUTORY_INVOICE_NUMBER_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) is raised if sequence generation exceeds 16 characters.
  - Financial year / calendar year reset prefix prevents sequence exhaustion across fiscal years.
  - Implemented statutory Credit Notes (`CN-YYYY-XXXX`) per **Rule 53 of the CGST Rules, 2017**, also guarded by the 16-character length limit.
  - Updated API route `src/app/api/invoices/[invoiceId]/route.ts` to support both singular `creditNote` (backwards compatibility) and `creditNotes` array (`1-to-many` relationship) reflecting full credit note adjustment history.

### Workstream D: Refund & Cohort Capacity Reconciliation
- **Root Cause Addressed:** Refunds must prevent double-decrementing batch capacity and protect academic completion history.
- **Architectural Fix Implemented:**
  - In `ZealoshDatabase.processRefund`:
    - Order state updated to `order.status = 'REFUNDED'` and `order.refundStatus = 'COMPLETED'`.
    - Batch seat capacity decrement is guarded: checks `enr.status === 'ACTIVE'` before decrementing, ensuring idempotent repeat calls or previously revoked enrollments do not double-decrement.
    - Clamped with `Math.max(0, batch.enrolledCount - 1)` ensuring capacity never drops below 0.
    - Statutory Credit Note generated and linked to original invoice.
    - In accordance with BDR-20, academic examination attempts, diagnostic breakdowns, and coursework records are preserved intact for regulatory compliance and audit trails.

### Workstream E: Payment Gateway Webhook Verification & Replay Protection
- **Root Cause Addressed:** Webhook processing must reject forged requests cryptographically and maintain durable replay protection.
- **Architectural Fix Implemented (`src/app/api/payments/webhook/route.ts`):**
  - **Step 1:** Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification executes first. Invalid signatures are rejected immediately with HTTP 400 before touching the database.
  - **Step 2:** Added durable `PaymentWebhookEvent` interface and `payment_webhook_events` database table. Incoming webhooks record `eventId`, `provider`, `eventType`, `payloadHash` (SHA-256), `receivedAt`, and `status`.
  - **Step 3:** Replay deduplication check detects duplicate event deliveries and returns HTTP 200 idempotently (`duplicateIgnored: true`) without re-enrolling or re-issuing invoices.
  - **Step 4:** Atomic order completion executes via `completePaymentOrder`. Upon success, event status transitions to `PROCESSED`; on error, transitions to `FAILED` with error diagnostics.
